A general overview of UnADM

In 2008, the Ministry of Public Education began work to create the Universidad Abierta y a Distancia de México (UnADM). The curriculum model for the academic programs and curricula was carried out in the first half of 2009, while a technological platform was developed to serve an initial population estimated at up to 12,000 students. The first call for applications for what was known as Programa de Educación Superior Abierta y a Distancia was published on August 12, 2009, with more than 34,000 applicants. On January 20, 2012, the UnADM was officially created by presidential decree. Sitio web en español: https://www.unadmexico.mx/nosotros/mision-vision-y-objetivos
